# Spec Driven Games — condition report, 15 September 2026

Written after implementing the "Sound Design & Retention Mechanics" spec
against the codebase. Two halves: what the game is right now, and what the
spec asked for versus what exists.

### Sound (spec §1) — was already built, now with a loudness stage

Everything is synthesised on one Web Audio graph (`lib/audio.ts`); there are
no audio files. Four buses with sliders (`ui`, `game`, `impact`, `music`),
voice caps, audio-clock scheduling, suspend on tab blur. Per mode: surface
footsteps with pitch jitter, jump/land/sprint wind; a checkpoint chime that
climbs a tone per gate; three-tier medal stingers plus an Author shimmer;
a personal-best fanfare; a panned ghost-pass whoosh; engine that pitches
with speed, drift, boost, lap bell, tiered podium; arena crunch, knockdown
bass + impact pause, match-point sting, countdown; a distinct kit per
cabinet; three ambient moods with an intensity layer driven by the run
clock. **Added today:** a compressor on the master (`-18 dB`, 3:1, soft
knee) so stacked peaks stay calm — the spec's "consistent loudness".

Not built, on purpose: menu music. The site is a web page first; music that
starts on the first click of a menu is the thing people mute.

## 2. What you have to do before this ships

1. **Apply the migration.** `drizzle/0005_retention_columns.sql` adds
   `players.daily_day`, `runs.week_ms`, `runs.week_key` and a partial index.
   All additive, all nullable or defaulted. Until it runs, `POST /runs` and
   the weekly board return 500 (`column "week_ms" does not exist`); the
   all-time board and everything else still work. `npx drizzle-kit migrate`.
2. **Redeploy the room server.** It has a new module (`rewards.ts`) and
   both rooms call it. No new environment: it reuses `APP_URL` and
   `MULTIPLAYER_SECRET`. Without the secret, matches are simply unpaid.
3. **Nothing else.** No new env vars, no new dependencies, no pinned
   versions touched.

## 3. Known condition, honestly

- **The room server sleeps.** Render free tier, ~a minute to wake. It is
  the single largest wait in the game and no code changes it — an uptime
  monitor does (see CLAUDE.md, Render section).
- **The characters are all the courier.** The licensed models are gone, so
  the shop's purchases are invisible on the rider and the runner. This is
  what blocks every cosmetic-reward item in the spec. Getting one licensed
  model back unblocks streaks, mastery tiers and skill-gated skins together.
- **The load is 1.1 MB gzipped of JavaScript**, most of it Rapier's inlined
  wasm. Prefetched from the menus; a direct `/race/<code>` link has nothing
  to prefetch from.
- **Sound is synthesised, not sampled.** That is a deliberate trade — no
  files, no licences, no decode on a phone — and the spec's "44.1 kHz
  16-bit sources" reads as a request for samples. The mix is calm and now
  limited; what it cannot be is a recording of a real tyre.
- **Coins are cosmetic and the guards say so.** Every payout rule in the
  code is explicit that it bounds farming, not forging. The floor guard on
  runs is the only anti-cheat and it protects the board, not the wallet.
