Creatures and bosses

Beast Tamer Arena has 31 animated 3D monsters: 19 elemental creatures you can tame and 12 giant bosses.

The 19 creatures

Beat a wild creature to grow its bond. Fill the bond and it joins your team of three. Each has its own fighting style, sounds and a signature Ultimate.

  • Bogwick

  • Bramblehulk

  • Brookling

  • Chimewisp

  • Cinderpup

  • Dromedrip

  • Duskmoth

  • Emberox

  • Galeowl

  • Gleamcalf

  • Gloomhare

  • Ivyram

  • Magmallow

  • Murkshell

  • Nebulynx

  • Rimefin

  • Starsprout

  • Sunskink

  • Thornkit

The 12 bosses

Every zone ends with a boss, and a VS intro opens each showdown. Bosses drop the cores used to evolve your creatures.

  • Astraxis

  • Baron Croakworth

  • Beacon Colossus

  • Dunemaw

  • Eclipse Warden

  • Glacier Wyrm

  • Kilnlord

  • Lantern Witch Moth

  • Sand Titan

  • Thornback Elder

  • Undertow Queen

  • Vaultkeeper Aurion

Elements: Ember burns Verdant, Verdant drinks Tide and Tide douses Ember, while Umbra and Aether hit each other hard.
