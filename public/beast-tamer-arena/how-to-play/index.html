How to play

Beast Tamer Arena is a real-time monster taming RPG. Here is how a run works.

Fight in real time

Steer your beast with one thumb. Dodge, lunge and fire its signature skill, and charge Surge for the finishing blow. When Surge lands, your creature unleashes its own Ultimate. Auto-attack and a movable control layout are available.

Tame what you beat

Beating a wild creature grows its bond. Fill the bond and the creature joins your team. There are no catch rolls, so no luck is involved.

Build a team of three

Train your creatures, evolve them with boss cores, equip gear and grow your trainer with a talent tree. Play to the elements: Ember burns Verdant, Verdant drinks Tide and Tide douses Ember, while Umbra and Aether hit each other hard.

Travel the Adventure map

Eight zones lead from Verdant Glade to Starfall Crater. Each zone has missions with waves, elite foes, boons to pick between waves and modifiers like Frenzy, Elite Hunt or Volatile, with a boss at the end.

More ways to play

  • Brawl: climb a ladder of rival teams in 3 vs 3 battles and pick a perk after each win.
  • Daily Challenge: the same seed and team for everyone today.
  • Boss Rush: hit this week's bosses as hard as you can and beat your record.
  • Egg Run: carry the egg to your nest. First to 3 wins.
  • The Today screen: a free daily tame, daily missions and camp rewards while you are away.

Fair by design

Every reward and price is shown before you choose. There are no loot boxes and nothing random for sale. Skins only change looks. Ads are optional and only appear when you ask for a bonus. The game plays offline, with optional Google Play Games sign-in.
