Beast Tamer Arena: Monster RPG

Tame monsters, evolve your team and win real-time battles in a 3D RPG. Offline!

Get it on Google Play

Tame monsters, build a team of three and win fast real-time battles in a 3D creature RPG you can play offline.

Beast Tamer Arena is a monster taming action RPG. Every fight happens in real time: steer your beast with one thumb, dodge, lunge, fire its signature skill and charge Surge for the finishing blow. When Surge lands, your creature unleashes its own Ultimate, with a unique animation and effect for each one.

NEW: FOUR NEW LANDS AND A STORY

Follow your mentor Rowan and a cast of new friends past Frostveil into four new zones: the night-lit Gloom Marsh, the floating Sky Ruins, the sandstorms of the Dune Sea and the cosmic Starfall Crater, where the star stag Astraxis waits at the end.

TAME THE MONSTERS YOU BEAT

Travel the Adventure map through eight zones, from Verdant Glade to Starfall Crater. Each zone holds missions with waves, elite foes, boons to pick between waves and modifiers like Frenzy, Elite Hunt or Volatile, with a boss at the end. Beat a wild creature and its bond grows. Fill it and the creature joins your team. No catch rolls, no luck.

31 ANIMATED 3D MONSTERS

Collect 19 elemental beasts, from Cinderpup and Brookling to the new Bogwick, Sunskink, Nebulynx and Starsprout, and face 12 giant bosses such as Baron Croakworth, Vaultkeeper Aurion, Dunemaw and the Glacier Wyrm. Each one has its own fighting style, sounds and signature Ultimate, and a VS intro opens every showdown.

LEVEL UP, EVOLVE AND GEAR UP

Train your creatures, evolve them with boss cores, equip gear and grow your trainer with a talent tree. Build your team around the elements: Ember burns Verdant, Verdant drinks Tide and Tide douses Ember, while Umbra and Aether hit each other hard.

3D HATCHERY AND RIVAL LEAGUE

From Trainer Level 5 the Hatchery is a real 3D egg machine with nests and upgrade stations. Each week you race four rivals, Rowan, Dash, Dr. Pell and Bartley, up the Bronze, Silver, Jade, Opal and Master leagues, and open new world tiers as you grow. Away for a while? You get a summary like "Rowan hatched 12 while you were away". Earnings keep coming while you are away (up to 8 h).

LIVING CAMP

Your camp is a living 3D scene where resting creatures sleep and breathe by day-night light.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY

  • Brawl: climb a ladder of rival teams in 3 vs 3 battles and pick a perk after each win.
  • Daily Challenge: the same seed and team for everyone today.
  • Boss Rush: hit this week's bosses as hard as you can and beat your record.
  • Egg Run: carry the egg to your nest. First to 3 wins.
  • The Today screen: a free daily tame, daily missions and camp rewards while you are away.

LOOKS FOR YOUR TEAM

Dress your favourites in skins like Frost Fox, Sakura, Ember Lava or Moonbeam. Skins only change looks, never power.

PLAY YOUR WAY

Auto-attack, simple controls, a movable control layout, haptics and a reduced-motion option. Tuned to run smoothly on older phones too.

FAIR BY DESIGN

  • Every reward and price is shown before you choose. No loot boxes, nothing random for sale, nothing that wins a fight.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: watch one only when you want a bonus. A limited full-screen ad (at most 3 a day) can show after a fight, never during one.
  • Plays offline. Optional Google Play Games sign-in with achievements and cloud save.
  • Quiet hours, play-time reminders and a monthly spend summary.
  • In English, Español, Português, Deutsch, Français, 日本語, 한국어 and 简体中文.
